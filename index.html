About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, headquartered in Chhattisgarh, India. The name "InAmigos" reflects our belief: "In friends, we find strength."

We operate across India addressing critical social challenges — hunger, lack of education, gender inequality, animal abuse, environmental degradation, and youth unemployment — through structured, community-first projects.

With certifications of 80G & 12A (tax exemption), CSR-1, NITI Aayog registration, and ISO 9001:2015 quality standards, we uphold the highest levels of transparency, accountability, and impact.

Social Impact

Why Our Work Matters

🍲

Fighting Hunger

Millions go hungry every day in India. Project SEVA fights food insecurity by reaching daily wage workers, homeless individuals, and underserved communities with warm meals and essential clothing.

🎓

Educating the Next Generation

BACHPANSHALA breaks barriers that prevent children from realizing their potential. Through digital tools and mentoring, we're preparing future leaders from underserved backgrounds.

💪

Empowering Women

UDAAN gives women in rural India the skills, confidence, and financial tools to lead independent lives. Gender equality is not just a goal — it's a right.

🌍

Protecting Our Planet

With 20,000+ saplings planted, PRAKRITI contributes directly to carbon offset, biodiversity, and climate resilience — one tree at a time.
